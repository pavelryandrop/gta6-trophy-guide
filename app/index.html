⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) may be missable: the first meditation is offered at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. Lucia mentions meditating in the kitchen one morning and walks to the balcony — follow her and accept. It cannot be repeated after Chapter 1.
